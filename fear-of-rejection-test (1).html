Fear of Rejection Test

From The Search for Significance by Robert S. McGee

McGee describes a false belief many of us live by: that we must be approved by certain others to feel good about ourselves. That belief breeds a fear of rejection that bends our attitudes and actions toward what others expect.

This short test helps you gauge how strongly that fear shapes your life. You'll see ten statements. For each, choose how often it's true of you, from Always to Never.

Answer honestly rather than how you wish you were. It takes about two minutes.
